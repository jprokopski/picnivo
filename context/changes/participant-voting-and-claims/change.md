# S-02: Participant voting and item claims

North-star slice. Turns the read-only public event page (`/e/{token}`) into the
interactive experience: participants join by name, vote Yes/Maybe/No on dates,
confirm attendance, claim/add items; the organizer selects the final date. The
page shows per-date tallies, the best date, item assignments, and the
participant list.

See `plan-brief.md` (start here) and `plan.md` (full plan).
